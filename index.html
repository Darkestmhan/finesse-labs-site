How a project actually runs, start to finish.

Four stages, roughly three to five weeks depending on scope. You'll always know what stage you're in.

01

Discovery call

Thirty minutes. We learn your business, your customers, and what the site actually needs to do — book appointments, sell a menu, show a portfolio.

02

Blueprint

You get a sitemap and a design direction before we build a single page. Nothing goes into production without your sign-off first.

03

Build

Two to four weeks of design and development, with two checkpoints along the way so you see progress instead of a surprise at the end.

04

Launch & handoff

Your site goes live, we record a short walkthrough video, and you get a plain-English guide for the changes you'll want to make yourself.

Questions we hear a lot.

If yours isn't here, ask us directly — see the contact section below.

Do I own the website once it's finished?+

Yes. You own the domain, the content, and the code. We build on standard, portable technology, so you're never locked into us to keep the site running.

What if I need changes after launch?+

Small edits are covered under your plan's support window. After that, you can go month-to-month on a Care Plan, or make changes yourself using the handoff guide we provide.

Do you handle hosting and domains?+

We'll set both up for you on reputable, affordable providers, or work with accounts you already own. Either way, you keep the login credentials.

How many revisions do I get?+

Two structured rounds of feedback at the Blueprint stage, and one round after the first build checkpoint. Most projects need less than that once the direction is approved.

What do you need from me to get started?+

Just time for a 30-minute call and any existing branding, photos, or copy you have. If you don't have those yet, we can help you pull them together as part of the project.
